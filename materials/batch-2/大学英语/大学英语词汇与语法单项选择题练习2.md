## 一、选择题（每小题 2 分，共 40 分）

### 选择 21

recent developments we do not think your scheme is practical. A) In view of B) In favor of C) In case of D) In memory of

答案：A。
参考解答：句意为“鉴于最近的发展，我们认为你的方案不切实际”。"In view of" 意为“鉴于，考虑到”，符合语境。"In favor of" 意为“支持，赞同”；"In case of" 意为“万一，如果发生”；"In memory of" 意为“纪念”。

### 选择 22

“This light is too for me to read by. Don't we have a brighter bulb some where"; said the elderly man. A) mild B) dim C) minute D) slight

答案：B。
参考解答：句意为“这灯太暗了，我没法读。我们有没有更亮的灯泡？”"dim" 意为“暗淡的”，形容光线不足，符合语境。"mild" 意为“温和的”；"minute" 意为“微小的”；"slight" 意为“轻微的”。

### 选择 23

It is said in some parts of the world, goats, rather than cows, serve as a vital of milk. A) storage B) source C) reserve D) resource

答案：B。
参考解答：句意为“据说在世界上的一些地方，山羊而不是奶牛，是牛奶的重要来源”。"source" 意为“来源”，常搭配 "serve as a source of..."。"storage" 意为“储存”；"reserve" 意为“储备”；"resource" 意为“资源”，但通常指自然资源或财力资源，此处强调产生牛奶的来源，用 source 更准确。

### 选择 24

Within two days, the army fired more than two hundred rockets and missiles at military in the coastal city. A) goals B) aims C) targets D) destinations

答案：C。
参考解答：句意为“两天内，军队向沿海城市的军事目标发射了两百多枚火箭和导弹”。"targets" 意为“目标”，特指被攻击或瞄准的对象。"goals" 和 "aims" 多指抽象的目标或目的；"destinations" 意为“目的地”。

知识点：[词汇与结构单项选择题练习(1)（选择1-20：词义辨析与语法结构）](../../../topics/大学英语/词汇结构单选1.md)

### 选择 25

Being ignorant of the law is not accepted as an \_ for breaking the law. A) excuse B) intention C) option D) approval

答案：A。
参考解答：句意为“不懂法不能成为违法的借口”。"excuse" 意为“借口，理由”，常用于 "as an excuse for doing sth."。"intention" 意为“意图”；"option" 意为“选择”；"approval" 意为“批准”。

### 选择 26

The London Marathon is a difficult race.\_\_ \_\_, thousands of runners participate every year. A) Therefore B) Furthermore C) Accordingly D) Nevertheless

答案：D。
参考解答：句意为“伦敦马拉松是一场艰难的比赛。尽管如此，每年仍有成千上万的跑步者参加。”前后句意存在转折关系，"Nevertheless" 意为“然而，不过”，表示转折。"Therefore" 和 "Accordingly" 表因果；"Furthermore" 表递进。

### 选择 27

### 选择 28

The chairman was blamed for letting his secretary too much work last week. A) take to B) take out C) take away D) take on

答案：D。

### 选择 29

It is clear that the dog has a much greater of its brain devoted to smell than is the case with humans. A) composition B) compound C) percent D) proportion

答案：D。
参考解答：句意为“很明显，狗的大脑中用于嗅觉的比例比人类大得多”。"proportion" 意为“比例，部分”。"composition" 意为“成分，作文”；"compound" 意为“化合物”；"percent" 意为“百分比”，前面通常不加冠词且不与 "of its brain" 这样搭配表示相对比例时不如 proportion 常用，且此处强调相对份额，proportion 更合适。

### 选择 32

The most successful post-career athletes are those who can take the skills they learned in sports and them to another area of life. A) utilize B) employ C) apply D) exert

答案：C。
参考解答：句意为“最成功的退役运动员是那些能够将他们在运动中学到的技能应用到生活其他领域的人”。"apply ... to ..." 意为“将……应用于……”。"utilize" 和 "employ" 虽也有利用之意，但搭配 "to another area" 时，apply 更强调迁移和应用。"exert" 意为“施加”。

### 选择 36

Europe's earlier industrial growth was \_\_\_\_\_ by the availability of key resources, abundant and cheap labor, coal, iron ore, etc. A) constrained B) detained C) remained D) sustained
