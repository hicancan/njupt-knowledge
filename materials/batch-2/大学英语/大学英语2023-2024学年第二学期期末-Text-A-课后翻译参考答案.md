# Unit 1–4 课后翻译参考答案

本资料由识别工具读取《大学英语》2023-2024 学年第二学期期末 Text A 课后翻译答案整理而成，已修正明显的识别错误（如断行、错字），并规范格式。

## Unit 1 课后翻译

### 选择 1

**中文原文：**
在日益现代化的社会，不丹独特的发展理念值得我们研究与深思。不丹是最后一个开放电视与网络的国家，手机和互联网的普及率 (penetration) 分别仅为 30% 和 5%，似乎与现代世界格格不入。在别的国家拼命发展经济，不惜代价提升 GDP 时，不丹追求的是国民幸福总值 (Gross National Happiness)。不丹认为幸福在于合理管理欲望，以实现物质与精神的平衡、现代性与传统性的平衡、经济增长与可持续发展的平衡。他们乐意吸取现代技术的益处，但按照自己的步伐，根据自己的需要，在自己觉得合适的时机去进行。他们同样渴望经济繁荣，但不会以牺牲他们所珍视的传统文化和环境可持续性为代价。

**英文译文：**
In an increasingly modernized society, Bhutan's unique development philosophy deserves our research and reflection. As the last country that is open to TV and the Internet, Bhutan's mobile phone and Internet penetrations are as low as 30% and 5% respectively, seemingly out of tune with the modern world. While other countries are striving to develop economy and boost GDP at all costs, what Bhutan pursues is GNH (Gross National Happiness). Bhutan believes that happiness consists in sensible restraint on desire to strike a balance between the material and the spiritual, modernity and tradition, economic growth and sustainable development. They are willing to take advantage of modern technology, but would do so at their own pace, according to their own needs and at their own proper time. Although likewise thirsting for economic prosperity, Bhutan refuses to realize it at the price of their cherished traditional culture and environmental sustainability.

## Unit 2 课后翻译

参考解答：**结论：**  
参考译文整体语义准确、结构清晰，但在若干用词、搭配、逻辑关系和语法细节上需要修改。

---

**逐句简评与修改建议：**

1. **“不丹是最后一个开放电视与网络的国家”**  
   原译：As the last country that is open to TV and the Internet  
   问题：`is open to` 表示“对……开放”，这里应指“开放电视与网络”这一政策动作，且“开放电视与网络”本身在中文里也不是最自然的搭配。  
   建议改为：the last country to open up to television and the Internet  
   或：the last country to allow television and the Internet

2. **“手机和互联网的普及率分别为 30% 和 5%”**  
   原译：Bhutan's mobile phone and Internet penetrations are as low as 30% and 5% respectively  
   问题：`penetrations` 用复数形式不常见，且 `as low as` 与 `respectively` 连用略显生硬。  
   建议改为：mobile phone and Internet penetration rates are only 30% and 5% respectively

3. **“在别的国家拼命发展经济，不惜代价提升 GDP 时”**  
   原译：While other countries are striving to develop economy and boost GDP at all costs  
   问题：`develop economy` 缺少冠词，应为 `develop the economy`；`striving to develop the economy and boost GDP` 可以，但 `striving` 不如 `spare no effort to` 更贴近“拼命”。  
   建议改为：While other countries spare no effort to develop their economies and boost GDP at all costs

4. **“不丹追求的是国民幸福总值”**  
   原译：what Bhutan pursues is GNH (Gross National Happiness)  
   问题：基本正确，可保留。

5. **“不丹认为幸福在于合理管理欲望”**  
   原译：Bhutan believes that happiness consists in sensible restraint on desire  
   问题：`restraint on` 搭配不自然，通常说 `restraint of desire` 或 `restraining desire`；“合理管理欲望”也不只是“克制”，还有“调节、引导”之意。  
   建议改为：happiness lies in properly managing desires  
   或：happiness consists in the sensible management of desire

6. **“以实现物质与精神的平衡、现代性与传统性的平衡、经济增长与可持续发展的平衡”**  
   原译：to strike a balance between the material and the spiritual, modernity and tradition, economic growth and sustainable development  
   问题：`strike a balance between A, B and C` 结构可以，但 `modernity and tradition`、`economic growth and sustainable development` 前面缺少必要的冠词或平行结构提示，读起来并列关系不够清楚。  
   建议改为：so as to achieve a balance between the material and the spiritual, between modernity and tradition, and between economic growth and sustainable development

7. **“他们乐意吸取现代技术的益处，但按照自己的步伐，根据自己的需要，在自己觉得合适的时机去进行。”**  
   原译：They are willing to take advantage of modern technology, but would do so at their own pace, according to their own needs and at their own proper time.  
   问题：意思基本正确，但 `at their own proper time` 略重复，`according to their own needs` 也可以简化为 `according to their needs`。  
   建议改为：They are willing to embrace the benefits of modern technology, but only at their own pace, according to their own needs, and when they deem it appropriate.

8. **“他们同样渴望经济繁荣，但不会以牺牲他们所珍视的传统文化和环境可持续性为代价。”**  
   原译：Although likewise thirsting for economic prosperity, Bhutan refuses to realize it at the price of their cherished traditional culture and environmental sustainability.  
   问题：`realize it` 中的 `it` 指代 economic prosperity，不如直接说 `achieve it`；`at the price of` 可以，但后面并列的两项中 `environmental sustainability` 缺少 `their` 修饰，稍显不平衡。  
   建议改为：They likewise desire economic prosperity, but they will not achieve it at the expense of their cherished traditional culture and environmental sustainability.

---

**修改后参考译文：**

In an increasingly modernized society, Bhutan’s unique development philosophy deserves our study and reflection. As the last country to open up to television and the Internet, Bhutan has mobile phone and Internet penetration rates of only 30% and 5% respectively, seemingly out of step with the modern world. While other countries spare no effort to develop their economies and boost GDP at all costs, what Bhutan pursues is Gross National Happiness. Bhutan believes that happiness lies in properly managing desire so as to achieve a balance between the material and the spiritual, between modernity and tradition, and between economic growth and sustainable development. They are willing to embrace the benefits of modern technology, but only at their own pace, according to their own needs, and when they deem it appropriate. They likewise desire economic prosperity, but they will not achieve it at the expense of their cherished traditional culture and environmental sustainability.

### 选择 2

**中文原文：**
中国的科举制度 (the imperial examination system) 始于隋朝，历经 1,300 多年。在中国古代，阶级意识 (class consciousness) 分明 (distinct)，下层社会鲜有机会在朝廷 (court) 占据一席之地。但自从引进科举制度后，寒门子弟 (scholars from poor families) 得以参加考试，有机会光宗耀祖 (bring honor to one's family)。这种人才选拔制度不仅对中国封建社会 (feudal society) 的政治、经济、教育、文化观念和社会风尚有重大影响，还影响到了日本、朝鲜、越南等周边国家的教育体制。在某种程度上，中国现行的教育体制也选择性地继承 (inherit) 了科举制度的一些优越之处。

**英文译文：**
The imperial examination system of China began in the Sui Dynasty and lasted more than 1,300 years. In ancient China, class consciousness was distinct and people from lower classes had little chance to gain a position in the official court. But once the imperial examination system was introduced, scholars from poor families had opportunities to take the government exams, which enabled them to bring honor to their families. Such a talent-selection system had great impact not only on the politics, economy, education, cultural concepts and social customs of Chinese feudal society, but also on the education systems of such neighboring countries as Japan, Korea and Vietnam. To some extent, China's current education system has selectively inherited some advantages of the imperial examination system.

## Unit 3 课后翻译

参考解答：**结论：** 选择 2（即所提供的英文译文）为正确参考译文。

**推理过程：**

1. **时态准确**：描述历史事实用一般过去时（`began`, `lasted`, `was distinct`, `had`, `was introduced`），末句“现行的……继承了”强调对现在的影响，用现在完成时 `has selectively inherited`，时态切换合理。
2. **句型结构得当**：
   - “不仅……还……”译为 `not only on ..., but also on ...`，介词 `on` 平行对称。
   - “得以参加考试，有机会光宗耀祖”处理为非限制性定语从句 `..., which enabled them to bring honor to their families`，避免流水句，符合英语形合特征。
3. **术语与搭配规范**：`class consciousness was distinct`（阶级意识分明）、`talent-selection system`（人才选拔制度）、`social customs`（社会风尚）、`selectively inherited`（选择性地继承）均为地道表达。
4. **语序调整**：“日本、朝鲜、越南等周边国家”译为 `such neighboring countries as Japan, Korea and Vietnam`，使用 `such ... as ...` 结构将中心词前置，符合英文习惯。

**其他选项不对的原因：** 其他选项通常在时态误用（如末句未用现在完成时）、`not only...but also` 结构不平行、或定语从句/分词修饰关系错误等方面存在硬伤。

### 选择 3

**中文原文：**
中国神话故事在西方人看来非常不可思议。面对滔滔洪水，大禹吸取父亲的教训，坚持 13 年，最终战胜水患。面对家门口耸立的高山，愚公的选择是带领全家老小移山而非搬家。面对火辣辣的 10 个太阳，后羿的选择是射落其中的 9 个，只留下一个照耀大地。这些神话故事本质上体现了中华民族的抗争精神—我命由我不由天。在中华文明的发展中，不屈不挠的抗争精神已经成为了一种文化基因。这是中华民族屹立至今的重要原因之一。

**英文译文：**
To Western people, Chinese myths seem to be exceptionally inconceivable. Confronted with raging floods, Yu, drawing lessons from his father's failure, eventually managed to tame the floods after 13 years of perseverance. Blocked by the big mountains, Yu Gong, instead of moving away, chose to remove the mountains with his family members. Scorched by the ten burning suns, Hou Yi opted to shoot down nine of them, leaving only one to shine upon the earth.

These mythical stories essentially embody the struggling spirit of the Chinese nation to have their fate in their own hands rather than that of gods. This unyielding struggling spirit has evolved into a gene of Chinese culture, which has contributed much to making China one of the longest-standing nations in human history.

## Unit 4 课后翻译

参考解答：## 参考译文

To Westerners, Chinese myths often seem extremely incredible. Faced with surging floods, Yu the Great learned from his father’s failure and persevered for thirteen years before finally conquering the floods. Confronted with the high mountains blocking his doorway, Yu Gong chose to lead his whole family in removing them rather than move his home. Faced with ten scorching suns, Hou Yi chose to shoot down nine of them, leaving only one to shine upon the earth. At their core, these myths embody the Chinese nation’s spirit of resistance—the belief that “my destiny is determined by myself, not by Heaven.” Throughout the development of Chinese civilization, this indomitable spirit of resistance has become a cultural gene and one of the important reasons why the Chinese nation has endured to this day.

## 简要说明

- **“大禹”**译为 **Yu the Great**，比单用 **Yu** 更明确；“愚公”保留拼音 **Yu Gong**，符合常见译法。
- **“面对……”**分别用 **Faced with** 和 **Confronted with** 处理，避免机械重复，同时保持三个事例的排比结构。
- **“我命由我不由天”**译为 **my destiny is determined by myself, not by Heaven**，准确传达“不屈从于天命”的含义。
- **“文化基因”**可直译为 **a cultural gene**；“中华民族屹立至今”不宜只译成 **China**，用 **the Chinese nation has endured to this day** 更贴切。
- 原译文中 **struggling spirit** 容易偏向“挣扎”，此处用 **spirit of resistance** 更能突出“抗争精神”；**rather than that of gods** 也不够准确，因为原文说的是“天”，并非特指诸神。

### 选择 4

**中文原文：**
传统中国绘画形成了一个独特的艺术流派 (school offine art)，无论风格还是技巧都与世界上其他艺术门类迴然不同。传统的日本绘画可能算是唯一的例外，但毋庸置疑的是它深受中国文化的影响。传统中国绘画最为人熟知的莫过于水墨画 (ink and washpainting)。中国人绘画采用毛笔蘸 (dip) 墨汁或颜料，灵巧地挥酒 (wield) 于纸上。画家用或深或浅、或浓或淡的线和点构成一幅图画。在优秀画家的手里，笔和墨非常具有表现力 (expressive)。正因为如此，它们不仅是绘画的工具，也被视为艺术追求的象征。

**英文译文：**
Traditional Chinese painting constitutes a unique school of fine art, a school that is distinctly different from any other fine art school in the world, either in style or in techniques. Traditional Japanese fine art may be the only exception, but there is no doubt that it is heavily influenced by Chinese culture. No other traditional Chinese painting is better known than ink and wash painting. The Chinese do paintings with brushes, dipping their brushes in ink or paint and then skillfully wielding them on the paper. Painters produce pictures with lines and dots — some heavy, some light, some deep, and some pale. In the hands of an outstanding painter, brushes and ink can be highly expressive. Because of this, they are not only treated as tools for drawing pictures, but also as symbols of artistic pursuit.

参考解答：选择 4

译文准确传达了原文的语义、逻辑关系及文化术语，且句式结构符合英语表达习惯。关键处理包括：“形成了一个独特的艺术流派”译为 “constitutes a unique school of fine art”，动词精准；“无论……还是……”用 “either...or...” 对应，逻辑清晰；“最为人熟知的莫过于”采用 “No other...is better known than...” 的比较级否定形式，地道还原最高级含义；“或深或浅、或浓或淡”以排比结构 “some heavy, some light, some deep, and some pale” 呈现，保留原文韵律感；“不仅……也……”用 “not only...but also...” 完整对应，确保并列关系无误。其他选项（未列出）通常存在术语误译、逻辑连接词缺失或句式生硬等问题，而本译文在忠实度与可读性之间取得最佳平衡。
