### 计算 1

(1) $f = -kv = m\dfrac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}t}$

$$\int_0^t \mathrm{d}t = \int_{v_0}^v -\frac{m}{k}\frac{\mathrm{d}v}{v}$$

$$t = -\frac{m}{k}\ln\frac{v}{v_0}$$

$$v = v_0 e^{-\frac{k}{m}t}$$

$$f = -kv = m\frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}t} = m\frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}x}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = mv\frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}x}$$

$$\int_0^h \mathrm{d}x = \int_{v_0}^0 -\frac{m}{k}\,\mathrm{d}v$$

$$h = \frac{m}{k}v_0$$

知识点：[速度、加速度的定义与微分关系](../../../topics/大学物理/速度与加速度定义.md)、[牛顿定律在变力问题中的应用（如 ma_t = -mg sinθ）](../../../topics/大学物理/牛顿定律应用.md)

### 计算 2

法向：$F - mg\cos\theta = \dfrac{mv^2}{R}$

$$F = mg\cos\theta + \frac{mv^2}{R}$$

切向：$mg\sin\theta = ma_\tau$

$a_\tau = g\sin\theta$
