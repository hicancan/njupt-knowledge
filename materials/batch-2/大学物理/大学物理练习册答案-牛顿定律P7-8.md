### 计算 9

(1) $f = - k \nu = m { \frac { d \nu } { d t } }$

$$
\int_ {0} ^ {t} d t = \int_ {v 0} ^ {v} - \frac {m}{k} \frac {d v}{v}
$$

$$
t = - \frac {m}{k} \ln \frac {v}{\mathrm{v} _ {0}}
$$

$$
\nu = \nu_ {0} e ^ {- \frac {k}{m} t}
$$

$$
f = - k v = m \frac {d v}{d t} = m \frac {d v}{d x} \cdot \frac {d x}{d t} = m v \frac {d v}{d x}
$$

$$
\int_ {0} ^ {h} d x = \int_ {v _ {0}} ^ {0} - \frac {m}{k} d v
$$

$$
h = \frac {m}{k} v _ {0}
$$

知识点：[速度、加速度的定义与微分关系](../../../topics/大学物理/速度与加速度定义.md)、[牛顿定律在变力问题中的应用（如 ma_t = -mg sinθ）](../../../topics/大学物理/牛顿定律应用.md)

### 计算 10

向下为正

$$
m g - F - k v = m a = m \frac {d v}{d t}
$$

$$
\int_ {v _ {0}} ^ {v} \frac {m d v}{m g - F - k v} = \int_ {0} ^ {t} d t
$$

$$
v = - \frac {m g - F - k v _ {0}}{k} + \frac {m g - F}{k}
$$
