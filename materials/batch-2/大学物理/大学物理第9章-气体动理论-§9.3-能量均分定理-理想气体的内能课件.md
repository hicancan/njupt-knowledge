$\mathtt { a } \mathtt { a }$ 分子的平均总动能为：

$$
\overline {{\mathcal {E}}} _ {t} = \overline {{\mathcal {E}}} _ {k t} + \overline {{\mathcal {E}}} _ {r t} = t \cdot \frac {1}{2} k T + r \cdot \frac {1}{2} k T = \frac {(t + r)}{2} k T
