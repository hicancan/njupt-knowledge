### 计算 4

题干：速度合成及质量计算...

**解题过程：**

$$
v = \frac{\sqrt{21}}{5} c = 0.917 c = 2.75 \times 10^{8} m/s
$$

$$
\Delta t' = \frac{\Delta t - \frac{u}{c^2} \Delta x}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}} = -7.63 \times 10^{-6} s
$$

$$
v_x' = \frac{v_x - u}{1 - \frac{u v_x}{c^2}} = \frac{-2.7 \times 10^{8} - 2.9 \times 10^{8}}{1 + \frac{2.7 \times 2.9 \times 10^{16}}{c^2}} = -2.995 \times 10^{8} m/s
$$

$$
m_A = \frac{m_0}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}} = \frac{m_0}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{2.9^2}{3^2}}} = 3.9 m_0
$$

$$
m_B = \frac{m_0}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}} = \frac{m_0}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{2.7^2}{3^2}}} = 2.3 m_0
$$

答案：见上式推导结果。
