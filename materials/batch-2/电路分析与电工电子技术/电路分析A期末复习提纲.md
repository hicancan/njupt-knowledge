### 谐振电路

- **RLC 串联谐振**：
    - 特点
    - 公式推导
    - 会求电路响应、品质因数 $Q$、带宽 $BW$、谐振频率 $\omega_0$
- **GCL 并联谐振**：
    - 特点
    - 公式推导
    - 实际并联谐振电路、加载回路分析

#### RLC 串联电路

$$
\text{谐振频率} \quad \omega = \omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}
$$

$$
\text{谐振时的电抗} \quad X = \omega_0 L - \frac{1}{\omega_0 C} = 0
$$

$$
\dot{U}_{R0} = R \dot{I}_0 = \dot{U}_s
$$

$$
\dot{U}_{L0} = j \omega_0 L \dot{I}_0 = j \frac{\omega_0 L}{R} \dot{U}_s = j Q \dot{U}_s
$$

$$
\dot{U}_{C0} = \frac{1}{j \omega_0 C} \dot{I}_0 = -j \frac{1}{\omega_0 R C} \dot{U}_s = -j Q \dot{U}_s
$$

$$
B = \omega_0 C - \frac{1}{\omega_0 L} = 0 \quad (\text{注：此处原文公式似为并联导纳虚部，串联通常看电抗})
$$

$$
\text{谐振时的电流} \quad \dot{I}_{C0} = j \omega_0 C \dot{U}_0 = j \frac{\omega_0 C}{G} \dot{I}_s = j Q \dot{I}_s \quad (\text{注：此公式对应并联谐振语境，串联谐振电流相同})
$$

$$
\dot{I}_{G0} = G \dot{U}_0 = \dot{I}_s
$$

$$
U_{L0} = U_{C0} = Q U_s = Q U_{R0} \quad (\text{电压谐振})
$$

$$
BW = \frac{f_0}{Q} = \frac{R}{2\pi L} \quad (\text{单位 Hz})
$$

$$
Q = \frac{\omega_0 L}{R} = \frac{1}{\omega_0 C R}, \quad BW = \frac{\omega_0}{Q} = \frac{R}{L} \quad (\text{单位 rad/s})
$$

#### GCL 并联电路

$$
\dot{I}_{L0} = \frac{1}{j \omega_0 L} \dot{U}_0 = -j \frac{1}{\omega_0 L G} \dot{I}_s = -j Q \dot{I}_s
$$

$$
I_{L0} = I_{C0} = Q I_S = Q I_{R0} \quad (\text{电流谐振})
$$

$$
Q = \frac{\omega_0 C}{G} = \frac{1}{\omega_0 L G}
$$
