## 3.1 空间直角坐标系与向量

### 计算 1

设向量 $\mathbf{v}$ 的方向余弦分别满足：
(1) $\cos \alpha = 0$
(2) $\cos \beta = 0$
(3) $\cos \alpha = \cos \beta = 0$

问这些向量与坐标轴或坐标面的关系如何？

- (1) $\cos\alpha=0 \Rightarrow \alpha=\frac{\pi}{2} \Rightarrow$ 与 x 轴垂直或平行 yOz 平面
- (2) $\cos\beta=0 \Rightarrow \beta=\frac{\pi}{2} \Rightarrow$ 与 y 轴垂直或平行于 zOx 平面
- (3) $\cos\alpha=\cos\beta=0 \Rightarrow \alpha=\beta=\frac{\pi}{2} \Rightarrow$ 与 z 轴平行或垂直于 xOy 平面

答案：见上述分析。

知识点：[向量的数量积（内积、点积）及坐标运算](../../../topics/线性代数/向量数量积.md)、[空间直角坐标系、向量的坐标表示、模与方向余弦](../../../topics/线性代数/空间直角坐标系与向量.md)

### 计算 2

已知两点 $M_{1}(4, \sqrt{2}, 1), M_{2}(3,0,2)$ , 计算向量 $\overrightarrow{M_{1}M_{2}}$ 的模、方向余弦和方向角，并求方向与 $\overrightarrow{M_{1}M_{2}}$ 一致的单位向量。

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overrightarrow {M _ {1} M _ {2}} &= (3 - 4, 0 - \sqrt {2}, 2 - 1) = (- 1, - \sqrt {2}, 1) \\
|\overrightarrow {M _ {1} M _ {2}}| &= \sqrt {(3 - 4) ^ {2} + (0 - \sqrt {2}) ^ {2} + (2 - 1) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {1 + 2 + 1} = 2 \\
\cos \alpha &= - \frac {1}{2}, \quad \cos \beta = - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}, \quad \cos \gamma = \frac {1}{2} \\
\alpha &= \frac {2\pi}{3}, \quad \beta = \frac {3\pi}{4}, \quad \gamma = \frac {\pi}{3} \\
\frac {\overrightarrow {M _ {1} M _ {2}}}{| \overrightarrow {M _ {1} M _ {2}} |} &= \frac {1}{2} (- 1, - \sqrt {2}, 1) = (- \frac {1}{2}, - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}, \frac {1}{2})
\end{aligned}
$$

答案：
模为 2；
方向余弦为 $\cos \alpha = - \frac {1}{2}, \cos \beta = - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}, \cos \gamma = \frac {1}{2}$；
方向角为 $\alpha = \frac {2\pi}{3}, \beta = \frac {3\pi}{4}, \gamma = \frac {\pi}{3}$；
单位向量为 $(- \frac {1}{2}, - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}, \frac {1}{2})$。

## 3.2 向量的乘法

知识点：[向量的数量积（内积、点积）及坐标运算](../../../topics/线性代数/向量数量积.md)

### 计算 1

设 $|\alpha| = 3, |\beta| = 2, \angle(\alpha, \beta) = \frac{2\pi}{3}$ , 计算:

(1) $(3 \alpha - 2 \beta) \cdot (\alpha + 2 \beta)$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(3 \alpha - 2 \beta) \cdot (\alpha + 2 \beta) &= 3 \alpha \cdot \alpha + 6 \alpha \cdot \beta - 2 \beta \cdot \alpha - 4 \beta \cdot \beta \\
&= 3 | \alpha | ^ {2} + 4 \alpha \cdot \beta - 4 | \beta | ^ {2} \\
&= 3 | \alpha | ^ {2} + 4 | \alpha | | \beta | \cos \frac {2\pi}{3} - 4 | \beta | ^ {2} \\
&= 3 \times 9 + 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times (- \frac {1}{2}) - 4 \times 4 \\
&= 27 - 12 - 16 = -1
\end{aligned}
$$

(2) $\alpha$ 在 $\beta$ 上的投影 $\Pr j_{\beta} \alpha$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Pr j_{\vec {\beta}} \vec {\alpha} &= | \vec {\alpha} | \cdot \cos \angle (\vec {\alpha}, \vec {\beta}) \\
&= 3 \cdot \cos \frac {2\pi}{3} = 3 \cdot (- \frac {1}{2}) = - \frac {3}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

答案：
(1) $-1$
(2) $-\frac{3}{2}$

知识点：[向量的数量积（内积、点积）及坐标运算](../../../topics/线性代数/向量数量积.md)、[空间直角坐标系、向量的坐标表示、模与方向余弦](../../../topics/线性代数/空间直角坐标系与向量.md)

### 计算 2

设 $| \alpha | = 3, | \beta | = 2, | \alpha + \beta | = 4$, 求 $| \alpha - \beta |$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\because | \vec {\alpha} + \vec {\beta} | = 4 &\Rightarrow (\vec {\alpha} + \vec {\beta}) \cdot (\vec {\alpha} + \vec {\beta}) = | \vec {\alpha} + \vec {\beta} |^2 = 16 \\
&\Rightarrow | \vec {\alpha} | ^ {2} + 2 \vec {\alpha} \cdot \vec {\beta} + | \vec {\beta} | ^ {2} = 16 \\
&\Rightarrow 9 + 2 \vec {\alpha} \cdot \vec {\beta} + 4 = 16 \\
&\Rightarrow 2 \vec {\alpha} \cdot \vec {\beta} = 3
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
| \vec {\alpha} - \vec {\beta} | ^ {2} &= (\vec {\alpha} - \vec {\beta}) \cdot (\vec {\alpha} - \vec {\beta}) \\
&= | \vec {\alpha} | ^ {2} - 2 \vec {\alpha} \cdot \vec {\beta} + | \vec {\beta} | ^ {2} \\
&= 9 - 3 + 4 = 10 \\
\therefore | \vec {\alpha} - \vec {\beta} | &= \sqrt{10}
\end{aligned}
$$

答案：$\sqrt{10}$

知识点：[向量的数量积（内积、点积）及坐标运算](../../../topics/线性代数/向量数量积.md)

### 计算 3

设 $| \alpha | = 3, | \beta | = 2\sqrt{6}, | \alpha \times \beta | = 7\sqrt{2}$, 求 $\alpha \cdot \beta$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
| \vec {\alpha} \times \vec {\beta} | &= | \vec {\alpha} | | \vec {\beta} | \sin \angle (\vec {\alpha}, \vec {\beta}) = 7\sqrt{2} \\
\therefore \sin \angle (\vec {\alpha}, \vec {\beta}) &= \frac {7\sqrt{2}}{3 \times 2\sqrt{6}} = \frac {7\sqrt{2}}{6\sqrt{6}} = \frac {7}{6\sqrt{3}} = \frac {7\sqrt{3}}{18}
\end{aligned}
$$

*(注：原题识别数据可能存在偏差，若按常见题目逻辑，此处应验证 $\sin^2 + \cos^2 = 1$)*

假设题目数据无误，继续推导：
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos \angle (\vec {\alpha}, \vec {\beta}) &= \pm \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 \angle (\vec {\alpha}, \vec {\beta})} \\
&= \pm \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{7\sqrt{3}}{18}\right)^2} = \pm \sqrt{1 - \frac{147}{324}} = \pm \sqrt{\frac{177}{324}} = \pm \frac{\sqrt{177}}{18}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\therefore \vec {\alpha} \cdot \vec {\beta} &= | \vec {\alpha} | | \vec {\beta} | \cos \angle (\vec {\alpha}, \vec {\beta}) \\
&= 3 \times 2\sqrt{6} \times \left( \pm \frac{\sqrt{177}}{18} \right) \\
&= \pm \frac{6\sqrt{6} \cdot \sqrt{177}}{18} = \pm \frac{\sqrt{1062}}{3} = \pm \sqrt{\frac{118}{3}}
\end{aligned}
$$

*(注：由于原始OCR识别结果中数字部分存在乱码如 "2 6", "7 2", "7 8 8" 等，上述计算基于对常见题型结构的修正尝试。若严格按原文 "sin... = 26/39" 等模糊数据，结果可能不同。此处保留逻辑框架，建议核对原题数字。)*

**修正说明**：观察原文片段 `sin L ... = 26/39` 和 `cos ... = +/- 15/39`，这暗示 $\sin \theta = \frac{26}{39} = \frac{2}{3}$, $\cos \theta = \pm \frac{15}{39} = \pm \frac{5}{13}$。
若以此为准：
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec {\alpha} \cdot \vec {\beta} &= | \vec {\alpha} | | \vec {\beta} | \cos \theta \\
&= 3 \times 2\sqrt{6} \times (\pm \frac{5}{13}) \quad (\text{注意：此处需确认 } |\beta| \text{ 是否为 } 2\sqrt{6} \text{ 或其他值以匹配分数})
\end{aligned}
$$
鉴于原文数据混乱，此处仅列出解题步骤结构。

答案：根据修正后的三角函数值计算点积。
