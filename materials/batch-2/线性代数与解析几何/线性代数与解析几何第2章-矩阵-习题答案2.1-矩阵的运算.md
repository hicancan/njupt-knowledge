## 2.1 矩阵的运算

### 计算 1

矩阵 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -3 & 2 & 0 \\ 4 & -2 & -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, B = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 2 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ , 且 $X + A = 2(B - X)$ , 求 $X$。

答案：
$$
\begin{aligned}
X &= \frac {1}{3} (2 B - A) \\
&= \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 2 & 2 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

### 计算 2

矩阵 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}, B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \\ -1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}, C = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 3 & 3 \\ 1 & 4 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ ，计算下列各式：

(1) $AB$ 和 $BA$

答案：
$$
\begin{aligned}
A B &= \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 2 & - 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{l l} 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \\ - 1 & 4 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{l l} 6 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 0 \end{array} \right) \\
B A &= \left( \begin{array}{l l} 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \\ - 1 & 4 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 2 & - 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 6 & 2 \\ - 1 & 2 & 9 \end{array} \right)
\end{aligned}
$$

(2) $B ^ {T} C + A$

答案：
$$
\begin{aligned}
B ^ {T} C + A &= \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 2 & - 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 4 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{l l l} - 1 & 3 & 3 \\ 1 & 4 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \end{array} \right) + \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 2 & - 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{array} \right) \\
&= \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 0 & 9 & 0 \\ 5 & 1 9 & 1 5 \end{array} \right) + \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 2 & - 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{array} \right) \\
&= \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 1 1 & - 1 \\ 5 & 2 0 & 1 7 \end{array} \right)
\end{aligned}
$$

### 计算 3

设 $A = (a _ {1}, a _ {2}, a _ {3}), B = \left( \begin{array}{l} b _ {1} \\ b _ {2} \\ b _ {3} \end{array} \right)$

(1) 求 ${AB},{BA}$ 。

答案：
$$
A B = (a _ {1}, a _ {2}, a _ {3}) \left( \begin{array}{l} b _ {1} \\ b _ {2} \\ b _ {3} \end{array} \right) = (a _ {1} b _ {1} + a _ {2} b _ {2} + a _ {3} b _ {3})
$$
$$
B A = \left( \begin{array}{l} b _ {1} \\ b _ {2} \\ b _ {3} \end{array} \right) (a _ {1}, a _ {2}, a _ {3}) = \left( \begin{array}{l l l} a _ {1} b _ {1} & a _ {2} b _ {1} & a _ {3} b _ {1} \\ a _ {1} b _ {2} & a _ {2} b _ {2} & a _ {3} b _ {2} \\ a _ {1} b _ {3} & a _ {2} b _ {3} & a _ {3} b _ {3} \end{array} \right)
$$

(2) 设 $a_{1}b_{1} + a_{2}b_{2} + a_{3}b_{3} = a$ ，求 $(BA)^{n}$ 。

答案：
由 (1) 知 $A B = a _ {1} b _ {1} + a _ {2} b _ {2} + a _ {3} b _ {3} = a$。
$$
\begin{aligned}
{(B A) ^ {n}} &= B (A B) ^ {n - 1} A = a ^ {n} B A \\
&= a ^ {n - 1} \left( \begin{array}{l l l} a _ {1} b _ {1} & a _ {2} b _ {1} & a _ {3} b _ {1} \\ a _ {1} b _ {2} & a _ {2} b _ {2} & a _ {3} b _ {2} \\ a _ {1} b _ {3} & a _ {2} b _ {3} & a _ {3} b _ {3} \end{array} \right)
\end{aligned}
$$
