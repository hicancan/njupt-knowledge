### 证明 8
证明：

（必要性）若 n 维向量组 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{n}$ 线性无关，

则 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots\alpha_{n}$ 与 $\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2},\cdots\varepsilon_{n}$ 等价，

从而 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots,\alpha_{n}$ 可以线性表示任意 n 维向量.

（充分性）若任意 n 维向量都可由 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{n}$ 线性表示，

则 $\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2},\cdots\varepsilon_{n}$ 可由 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots\alpha_{n}$ 线性表示，

又 $\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2},\cdots\varepsilon_{n}$ 可线性表示 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots,\alpha_{n}$ ,

所以 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots,\alpha_{n}$ 与 $\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2},\cdots\varepsilon_{n}$ 等价

$$ R \left(\alpha_ {1}, \alpha_ {2}, \dots \alpha_ {n}\right) = R \left(\varepsilon_ {1}, \varepsilon_ {2}, \dots \varepsilon_ {n}\right) = n $$

所以 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots,\alpha_{n}$ 线性无关。
